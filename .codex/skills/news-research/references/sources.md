# Source Registry

Use the smallest source set that covers the requested axis and period.

| Country | Need | Primary source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| KR | Rates, monetary policy, financial stability | Bank of Korea monetary-policy releases |
| KR | Company earnings, guidance, disclosures | DART for held or watched companies |
| KR | Policy, fiscal measures | Ministry of Economy and Finance release |
| US | Rates and policy | FOMC statement and projections |
| US | Employment and CPI | BLS release |
| US | GDP, PCE, income and spending | BEA release |
| US | Company earnings, guidance, disclosures | Company IR or SEC filing |
| Both | Material event outside primary releases | One Reuters market wrap, then verify the cited primary source when possible |

## Five-day review

1. Check release calendars first; open only releases published in the window.
2. Check held or watched companies only when they published a filing or earnings release.
3. Use at most one market wrap per country/day, and only when a material event has no primary release.
4. Do not create a record for a quiet day.
